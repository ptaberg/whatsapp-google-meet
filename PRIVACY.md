# Privacy Policy: Meet Button for WhatsApp Web

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Meet Button for WhatsApp Web adds a button to WhatsApp Web that creates a Google Meet link and places it in the message box of the open chat.

## What the extension accesses

The extension works only in your browser and accesses:

- **The WhatsApp Web message box**, to insert the link and check that it was added.
- **Your sent messages in the open chat**, to detect that the link was sent, so the meeting can open.
- **The address of a temporary `meet.google.com` tab**, to read the new meeting link.

## What the extension does with it

The new link and meeting code are held in memory for up to five minutes while the extension waits for you to send the message. They are not saved.

The extension does not collect, store, sell or share your data. It has no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own, and it sends nothing to the developer.

## Other services

Creating or joining a meeting uses your own Google account and is covered by Google's terms and privacy policy. A link you choose to send is delivered by WhatsApp and is covered by WhatsApp's terms and privacy policy.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published on this page. For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/ptaberg/whatsapp-google-meet/issues.
